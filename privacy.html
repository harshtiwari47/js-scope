Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Client-Side Code Execution

JS-Scope runs entirely inside your browser client environment. Any code written in the CodeMirror editor is parsed and executed locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your code snippets on any external server.

2. Advertising & Cookies

JS-Scope uses third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Third-party vendors may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on your visit to this website and other sites on the Internet.

3. Third-Party Analytics

We may use anonymous analytics tools to monitor website performance and usage metrics. No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected.
